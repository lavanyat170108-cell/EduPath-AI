# 🚀 EduPath AI Development Roadmap

> Vision: Build an AI-powered Student Success & Analytics Platform that helps teachers and students transform academic data into meaningful insights, personalized recommendations, and continuous improvement.

---

# 🌟 Product Vision

EduPath AI is not a Student Management System.

It is an AI-powered Student Success Platform.

The goal is to help teachers identify students who need support early and help students improve through AI-driven guidance.
